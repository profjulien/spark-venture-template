# Experiments

One card per experiment, numbered: `01-short-name.md`, `02-…`.
`/experiment` writes each card and reads it on its read date.

A card holds:

- the assumption it tests, and why that one;
- the nine buckets, with the segment and problem assumption copied
  unchanged from `CLAUDE.md`;
- the arithmetic: how many people to ask, and the road to the milestone;
- the send plan: who sends what, where, by when, ticked as it goes;
- the result: asked, responded, committed, paid, and what changes next.

One card runs at a time. Its metric is the *Metric this week* line in
`project-state.md`.
